Overview

Big-farm power, small-farm size.

BhoomiShakti X1 helps small and marginal farmers with affordable, cleaner and efficient soil-ploughing operations.

The problem

Small farms are left out of mechanization

  • High fuel costs
  • Labour dependency
  • Expensive agricultural machinery
  • Limited access to suitable equipment
  • Large machines are hard to use on small farmland
The solution

A compact, solar-assisted, battery-powered machine

The solar panel assists battery charging while the battery powers the electric drive — providing affordable, cleaner and efficient soil ploughing.
