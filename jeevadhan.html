Overview

From a waste challenge to rural value.

A proposed circular biomass-to-resource ecosystem connecting livestock, farmers, rural biomass, renewable energy, agriculture and digital technology.

The problem

Rural biomass is underutilized

  • Unorganized cattle-dung collection
  • Limited biomass utilization
  • Dependence on conventional energy sources
  • No organized digital biomass network
  • Need for sustainable organic-resource management
  • Lost opportunity to recover biological resources for agriculture
The solution

A circular biomass-to-resource ecosystem

Organized biomass collection, processing through anaerobic digestion, a renewable-energy pathway and appropriate use of digestate — with farmer participation, digital monitoring and transaction records.

How can locally available cattle biomass be organized, processed and converted into useful renewable energy and agricultural resources while creating value for rural communities?

The circular system

Cattle → biomass → energy + digestate → farm ↺

How it works

Six proposed steps.

From farmer registration to digital monitoring.

  1. 01

    Farmer registration

    Farmers or livestock owners register: profile, livestock details, biomass availability and collection location.

    Digital prototype concept
  2. 02

    Biomass collection

    Farmers provide cattle dung to a village-level collection network. Record: farmer → quantity → collection → payment / transaction.

    Proposed
  3. 03

    Biomass processing

    Biomass is transported to an appropriate processing / biogas facility for anaerobic digestion.

    Proposed
  4. 04

    Renewable gas production

    Anaerobic digestion produces biogas. Biomethane / CBG would need purification, upgrading, compression and certified infrastructure.

    CBG: future scope
  5. 05

    Agricultural by-product

    Digestate / bio-slurry is processed and evaluated for appropriate agricultural use.

    To be evaluated
  6. 06

    Digital monitoring

    Track biomass collected, collection requests, processing status, energy and digestate records, and farmer transactions.

    Digital prototype concept
Biomass to energy — and to agriculture

One process, two pathways.

Anaerobic digestion produces both an energy pathway and an agricultural-resource pathway.

Implementation & feasibility

A staged development model.

Each phase validates the next. Nothing beyond the concept is claimed as built.

01

Phase 1 — Digital prototype

  • Farmer & livestock registration
  • Biomass availability
  • Collection request & tracking
  • Farmer, admin & processing dashboards
02

Phase 2 — Pilot implementation

  • Selected villages → collection centres
  • Biomass aggregation → biogas facility
  • Energy + digestate
  • Measure: availability, logistics, processing, energy output, digestate, operating costs
03

Phase 3 — Scale

  • Village → cluster → district → regional network
  • After technical and economic validation
  • Requires technical, environmental, safety and regulatory approvals
