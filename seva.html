Overview

Less time searching. More time farming.

The problem

Finding machines and people is hard

  • Tractors, mini tractors, JCBs and harvesters are hard to find in time
  • Farm labour and loading/unloading workers are hard to arrange
  • Transport vehicles depend on personal contacts
  • Discovery relies on phone calls, intermediaries and personal networks
  • Unclear equipment availability, provider information and pricing
The solution

One digital service platform

Farmers discover and request agricultural services on one platform — from machinery and labour to transport and other farm services — with verified providers, simple payments and ratings.

BhoomiShakti Seva is the service connectivity layer of the BhoomiShakti ecosystem.
