Overview

Everything a farmer looks for, in one place.

The problem

A digital connectivity gap

  • Hard to discover reliable agricultural businesses
  • Hard to find quality products and services
  • Market information and government schemes are scattered
  • Agricultural knowledge and opportunities are spread out
  • Local agricultural businesses have limited digital reach to farmers
The solution

One digital agriculture platform

Farmers explore crops, businesses, products, services, agricultural information, prices and demands — then submit enquiries and use the agriculture-focused AI assistant.

Businesses reach farmers, showcase products and services, receive enquiries, discover demand and manage their digital presence.

Business connection workflow

A two-sided ecosystem, connected.

Both sides meet at the enquiry and the business response.

Farmer side
  1. 01

    Farmer

    Starts the search.

  2. 02

    Discover business

    District / taluk-aware discovery.

  3. 03

    View products / services

    Profiles with products and services.

  4. 04

    Compare

    Compare options and prices.

  5. 05

    Send enquiry

    Direct digital enquiry.

  6. 06

    Business response → Connection

    The farmer and business connect.
