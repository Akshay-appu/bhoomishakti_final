Tool 1 · Weather & spray advisor

Know the week before you work.

Search your village or use your current location to see a 7-day forecast with simple, cautious guidance for irrigation, field work and spraying.

Tip: add the district or state after a comma if there are many places with the same name, for example “Hosakote, Karnataka”.

Forecast for

How this guidance is worked out
  • Rain expected when the forecast for the day is 2.5 mm or more — the India Meteorological Department’s lower limit for “light rain” in 24 hours.
  • Very hot when the forecast maximum is 40 °C or more — the IMD’s heat-wave threshold for the plains (hill areas use a lower threshold).
  • Possible spray hours are daytime hours (6 AM – 6 PM) with no rain forecast and wind between 5 and 13 km/h (3–8 mph), the range recommended in pesticide-stewardship guidance. Very calm air can also cause drift.
  • Forecast wind is at 10 m height and for your area, not your exact field.

Sources: India Meteorological Department (rainfall terminology, heat-wave definition) · Pesticide Environmental Stewardship — evaluate the weather

Weather-based guidance is informational. Always consider local field conditions, crop requirements and product-label instructions before spraying.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com · Only the place name or location you choose is sent to get the forecast. Nothing is stored.

      Tool 4 · Government schemes & helplines

      Official help, in one place.

      Links go only to official Government of India websites. Helpline numbers are shown only where an official source confirms them — each one shows its source.

      Official Government Resource

      PM-KISAN

      Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

      Central government income-support scheme for farmer families. Registration, eKYC and beneficiary status are handled on the official portal.

      15526124×7 IVRS helpline · Source: PIB, Government of India ↗
      Visit pmkisan.gov.in
      Official Government Resource

      PMFBY

      Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (crop insurance)

      Government-sponsored crop insurance scheme. The Krishi Rakshak Portal & Helpline supports claims, grievances and queries.

      14447Krishi Rakshak Portal & Helpline · Source: PIB, Government of India ↗
      Visit pmfby.gov.in
      Official Government Resource

      Soil Health Card

      Soil Health Card Scheme

      A report on your soil’s nutrient status with fertiliser and soil-amendment recommendations. The portal is used to view and print the card.

      Visit soilhealth.dac.gov.in
      Official Government Resource

      eNAM

      National Agriculture Market

      Online trading platform for agricultural produce that links mandis (APMCs) across states.

      1800 270 0224Toll-free helpline · Source: enam.gov.in ↗
      Visit enam.gov.in
      Official Government Resource

      Kisan Call Centre

      Kisan Call Centre (KCC)

      Answers farmers’ questions on the phone in their own language, through agriculture specialists. Available 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM, all seven days.

      1800-180-1551Toll-free · Source: mkisan.gov.in ↗
      Visit mkisan.gov.in

      Eligibility, benefits and application steps can change. Always check the official website or helpline for the latest details. BhoomiShakti is not affiliated with these government services. Information verified in October 2026.

      Farmer safety & information note

      • These tools support your decisions; they do not replace advice from your local agriculture officer, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or the Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551).
      • Before spraying, read and follow the product label and wear the protective equipment it asks for.
      • Weather forecasts can change. Check again on the day of work.
      • The land converter and profit planner run only in your browser. BhoomiShakti does not receive or store what you type.
