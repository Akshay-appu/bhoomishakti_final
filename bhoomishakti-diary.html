What it records

Everything That Happens on the Farm, Remembered.

Ten simple record types cover a farmer’s whole working day — no forms full of jargon.

Sowing

Which crop, which field, which day — the start of every season.

Irrigation

Each watering, so you know when the field last got water.

Crop Activities

Weeding, thinning, staking and every task in between.

Farm Inputs

Seeds, fertiliser and sprays — what was used and when.

Labour

Who worked, for how long, and what was paid.

Machinery

Ploughing, sowing and harvesting work with machines.

Expenses

Every rupee spent on the farm, in one list.

Soil Activities

Manure, compost, soil tests and soil care.

Harvest

How much was harvested, and from which field.

Farm Notes

Anything worth remembering — in your own words.

Ideas we are exploring

Each idea starts with a real farmer problem.

Today on My Farm

Farmer problem: Farmers juggle many tasks and forget what is pending.

Idea: One screen shows today’s records, days since sowing and what was planned.

One-Tap Record

Farmer problem: Typing in the field is slow, with dirty hands and harsh sunlight.

Idea: Big picture buttons record the most frequent activities in one or two taps.

Crop Memory

Farmer problem: With several crops, records get mixed up.

Idea: Every record is filed under its crop and field automatically.

Season Book

Farmer problem: Paper notebooks get lost or damaged.

Idea: A complete, printable record of the whole season.

Farm Passport

Farmer problem: Farm history is scattered across years and notebooks.

Idea: Season books stack into a long-term history of the same land.

Share My Farm Record

Farmer problem: Explaining past work to family, buyers or officers is hard.

Idea: The farmer chooses which records to share — nothing is shared automatically.

Voice Notes

Farmer problem: Not every farmer is comfortable typing.

Idea: Speak a short note in your own language and keep it with the record.

Offline-First

Farmer problem: Mobile signal in the field is weak or missing.

Idea: Records save on the phone first and work without internet.
