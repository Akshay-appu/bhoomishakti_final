Overview

Feed the soil, not just the crop.

A sustainable model combining organic nutrient management, controlled bio-nitrogen inputs, soil biological activity and mechanized soil incorporation.

The problem

Overdependence on synthetic fertilizers

  • Soil degradation and acidification
  • Nutrient imbalance
  • Reduced soil biodiversity
  • Decline in earthworm populations
  • High labour requirement for organic farming
  • Uneven nutrient distribution
The solution

A controlled bio-nutrient system + X1

Aged manure, compost, soil, cocopeat and diluted urine are prepared as a controlled bio-nutrient mixture, stabilized, then mixed into the soil using BhoomiShakti X1.

Objectives

  • Develop a controlled bio-nitrogen system
  • Improve soil biological activity and support earthworms
  • Enhance sustainable crop productivity
  • Reduce dependence on chemical fertilizers
  • Reduce manual soil mixing with BhoomiShakti X1
How JeevaDhara works — in simple terms

Prepare. Rest. Mix in.

“We prepare an organic nutrient mixture, allow it to stabilize, and then use BhoomiShakti X1 to mix it into the soil more efficiently.”

1 · Prepare

Mix manure, compost, soil and cocopeat, then add diluted urine slowly.

2 · Stabilize

Let the moist mixture rest for 24–48 hours.

3 · Mix into soil

BhoomiShakti X1 incorporates the mixture into the soil.
